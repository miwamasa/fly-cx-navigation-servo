What this demo computes, and what it does not

A real circuit model, not a cartoon

Each fly runs the full 2,308-neuron rate model of the MaleCNS central complex in 20 ms steps. The goal is injected into FC2, self-motion enters through the noduli (LNO, SpsP), and steering is read from the left–right difference of PFL3. The body turns at 2.6 rad/s × u. Nobody tells the added circuit the true travel direction.

What the added circuit is

Paper B, section 7: (1) a multiplicative gate on PFN, (2) non-negative weights restricted to PFN → hΔB pairs that exist in the connectome, and (3) a squaring comparator in PFL3, all calibrated once on training conditions and then frozen. Variant N reads angles out of population activity. Variant C uses only cell activities, plus a newly declared hΔB → PFL3 projection, because the connectome has only 32 synapses there. These are declared mechanisms, not findings about the fly.

Numbers in the paper

Over all 12 reference trials, the median travel error is 42.6° for the plain circuit, 17.7° with variant N and 14.8° with variant C. One trial on screen can differ from that median. The browser reproduces the Python run of every reference trial to within 0.01° (checked by tests/test_servo_demo.mjs).

The task

Hold a fixed goal direction while the sideslip pushes the fly sideways. Travel direction T = heading H + sideslip φ. The paths are drawn by integrating the travel velocity; position is not fed back. There is no maze, no obstacles and no fluid dynamics. 0° is to the right and 90° is up.
